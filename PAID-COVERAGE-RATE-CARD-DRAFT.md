## THE RULES (non-negotiable, and they're the selling point)
1. Paid coverage is labeled **Sponsored** — on the post, in the story, in the
   video description. Always. That's the law and it's why the audience trusts it.
2. Facts get verified like any other story. We don't print claims we can't check,
   and we don't write fake reviews — coverage is coverage, not a guaranteed rave.
3. Charles has final say on fit. If it's not for this audience, we pass and
   refund before work starts.
4. Payment up front through Gumroad. Standard turnaround 72 hours from
   receiving your assets (artwork, links, facts sheet).
