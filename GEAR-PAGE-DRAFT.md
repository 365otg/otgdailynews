## THE O.T.G STUDIO — GEAR I ACTUALLY USE

No rented rooms, no borrowed setups. This is the exact chain the beats, the
tutorials, and the archive are made on — most of it filmed start to finish on the
Paris Eighty channel.

### THE CHAIN
Instruments feed the **Stones Throw SP-404MKII** (end of chain — the effects and
the final sound) → its output runs into the AUX input on the **RØDECaster Pro** →
the RØDECaster feeds the speakers and records the whole mix, plus the mic,
straight to the computer over USB.
