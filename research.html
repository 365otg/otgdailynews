OTG Research Report

The OTG Research Report

A clean-slate, longitudinal look at U.S. mass shootings going back to 1966 — “what happens before, during and after,” and what is missing from the coverage. Sources are posted so you can check them.

Before

Who the shooter was, the incident history, and the precursor timeline — the warning signs, the systems around them, and what was known before the day.

During & After

What happened on the day, the response, and the aftermath — built event by event, not from a single headline.

Each chapter focuses on one incident. The pages live on the Over Time Grind archive, and the report is written so a reader can follow the whole timeline alone — when someone books the report for a class, they are taken through it in full.

Corrections: if a source contradicts a chapter, we print the correction. Tell us on the blog post for that chapter, where comments live.
