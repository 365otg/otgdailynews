The O.T.G Studio — Gear I Actually Use

No rented rooms, no borrowed setups. This is the exact chain the beats, the tutorials, and the archive are made on — most of it filmed start to finish on the Paris Eighty channel. Every item is gear Charles has actually owned and used on camera.

The Chain

Instruments feed the Stones Throw SP-404MKII (end of chain — the effects and the final sound) → its output runs into the AUX input on the RØDECaster Pro → the RØDECaster feeds the speakers and records the whole mix, plus the mic, straight to the computer over USB.
