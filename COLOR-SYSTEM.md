# O.T.G COLOR SYSTEM — v1 (drafted Nov 7, 2026)

One master palette, one accent per lane. Every graphic, thumbnail, page, and video
package pulls from this file only. Max 3 colors per graphic. OTG branding only —
never a source outlet's colors, name, or logo.

## MASTER — O.T.G (top tier, all lanes inherit)

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Base | OTG Black | #0A0A0A |
| Primary accent | OTG Crimson | #C8102E |
| Secondary accent | OTG Gold | #C9A227 |
| Soft accent | Archive Tan | #D6C08A |
| Paper | OTG Cream | #F5EFE0 |
| Type on dark | White | #FFFFFF |

Master rules (already locked):
- Headlines: Anton. News hooks: BLACK letters on SOLID WHITE blocks, one block per line.
- Watermark "OVER TIME GRIND • @SupplyTheWork • @ParisEighty" — small, Archive Tan,
  directly UNDER the gold divider. Never at the croppable bottom edge.
- Take lines in Crimson, stat/linger lines in Gold/Tan (v2 verdict, Nov 6).

## THE LANES

### OTG Daily News (the newscast)
Black base · Crimson primary · White type · Gold divider.
Breaking energy. This is the default look for news graphics and the nightly show.

### The OTG Research Report (documentaries)
Steel Blue #2E4A62 primary · Silver #C0C6CE type/lines · Gold hairline.
The serious, document feel — court records, archives, history. Thumbnail bug:
OTG RESEARCH REPORT. (Steel/silver/gold already proven on the Keefe D samples.)

### The OTG News Special Report (event/breaking long treatment)
Crimson-dominant · Black · White. Reserved — if everything is urgent, nothing is.
Only for stories big enough to interrupt the schedule.

### OTG Morning Brief
Cream base · Gold primary · Black type. Dawn look — light background, the only
lane that leads with Cream. Weather + headlines + the day's history.

### OTG Nightly News
Deep Navy #0B1D33 base · Silver type · Crimson accent.
Evening counterpart to the Morning Brief — same show, after dark.

### OTG Music
Gold-dominant · Black · Deep Purple #3B1F5E accent.
Beat videos, album stories, the 24/7 music blocks. Gold = the catalog.

### OTG Weather
Steel Blue #2E4A62 · White · Gold for warnings/severe.
Clean and legible at a glance — weather never competes with the news look.

## USAGE RULES
1. One lane = one accent story. Don't mix lane palettes inside a single graphic.
2. Faces and photos stay natural color — duotone grading only where the locked
   doc-thumbnail style calls for it.
3. Red is never used on a deceased person's name/headline treatment (Sanft rule).
4. New lane or new product? It gets an accent from this file or a proposed
   addition here first — no freestyle colors in the wild.
